Privacy Policy Terms & Conditions

Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Remozy - Remote for Samsung TV (package com.tvremote.samsungtv; "the App", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use it. The App is developed by Maulik J. Bhanderi, publishing as NacXo, India. By installing and using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of the App.

Summary

  • No account is needed, and we never ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • TV pairing details stay on your device.
  • The App is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob collects some device data to show and measure ads, as described below.

Information Stored on Your Device

  • Device pairing data: When you connect to your Samsung TV, the App stores the TV's name, local IP address and pairing token on your device so it can reconnect quickly. This information is not sent to us.
  • Local network communication: Remote commands (power, volume, channels, navigation, app launch, casting) travel directly between your phone and your TV over your local Wi-Fi network.

Information Collected by Google AdMob

The App uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to display ads. AdMob may automatically collect the following data and share it with Google:

  • Device or other IDs (Android advertising ID): to show and measure ads, for analytics and fraud prevention.
  • Approximate location (derived from your IP address): to show relevant ads, for analytics and fraud prevention.
  • App interactions (for example ad views and taps): to show and measure ads, and for analytics.
  • Crash logs and diagnostics: for analytics and fraud prevention.

This data is encrypted in transit. We do not receive it in a form that identifies you personally. Google's use of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Permissions

The App may request the following permissions to function correctly:

  • Internet / Wi-Fi / local network access: To find and control your Samsung TV, cast media, and load ads.
  • Advertising ID: Used by AdMob as described above.

Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Apart from the AdMob data described above, we do not share data with third parties except when required by law or to respond to valid legal processes.

Your Choices

  • Reset or delete your advertising ID: Android Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Settings → Google → Ads).
  • Limit personalised ads: Use the same Android setting, or Google's My Ad Center.
  • Clear pairing data: Clear the App's storage or uninstall the App.

Data Retention and Security

Pairing data stays on your device until you clear it. Data collected by AdMob is retained by Google under its own policies. We take reasonable measures to protect any information processed by the App, but no method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can help remove it.
